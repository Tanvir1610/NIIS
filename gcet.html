About GCET

The Gujarat College of Engineering and Technology (GCET) is a prestigious institution of engineering education located in Vallabh Vidyanagar, Gujarat, India. Established under the aegis of Charutar Vidya Mandal (CVM), GCET is committed to delivering quality engineering education and fostering research and innovation.

With state-of-the-art facilities, experienced faculty, and a strong emphasis on practical learning, GCET has emerged as a center of excellence in engineering education. The institution actively supports research initiatives and promotes collaboration with leading universities and industries worldwide.
