About the Conference

The NIIS (National Conference on Integrated and Intelligent Systems) 2026 is an international forum dedicated to advancing research, innovation, and practice in integrated and intelligent systems. The conference brings together researchers, practitioners, and industry experts to share cutting-edge developments across multiple domains.

NIIS 2026 will be hosted by the Gujarat College of Engineering and Technology (GCET) in Vallabh Vidyanagar, India, and is supported by leading academic institutions and industry partners globally.

Key Features

Global Reach

Participants from 40+ countries contributing research and insights on integrated systems technology

Research Focus

Cutting-edge research papers, technical presentations, and industry case studies on intelligent systems

Networking

Connect with leading researchers, industry professionals, and innovation leaders worldwide

Innovation

Explore the latest innovations in AI, IoT, embedded systems, and smart technologies

Keynote Speakers

Learn from renowned experts and thought leaders through inspiring keynote presentations

Recognition

Outstanding papers and contributions recognized with awards and publication opportunities

Why Attend NIIS 2026?

  • Submit and publish research in peer-reviewed conference proceedings
  • Attend keynote sessions from industry leaders and renowned researchers
  • Participate in workshops on emerging technologies and research trends
