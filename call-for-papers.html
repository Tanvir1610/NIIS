NIIS 2026 - International Conference

Join us in presenting cutting-edge research in Networking, Communication, and Computing Technology.

Conference Overview

NIIS 2026 is an international conference where theory, practices, and applications of Networking, Communication, and Computing Technology and related topics are presented and discussed. Original contributions are solicited on topics covered under broad areas such as (but not limited to):

Submission Requirements

English is the official language of the conference. We welcome paper submissions. Prospective authors are invited to submit full (original) research papers which are NOT submitted, published, or under consideration anywhere in other conferences or journals. Papers must be submitted in electronic format (Doc or Docx) through the CMT Conference Management System only.
