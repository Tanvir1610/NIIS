Submit Your Research to NIIS 2026

We invite researchers, practitioners, and industry experts to submit their original research papers on cutting-edge topics in integrated and intelligent systems.

Paper Submission Guidelines

Scope and Topics

NIIS 2026 welcomes research papers covering a wide range of topics related to integrated and intelligent systems. Papers should present original research, novel methodologies, or significant extensions of existing work.

Research areas include but are not limited to:

  • Energy, Communication, and Sensing Systems
  • Embedded Systems and IoT
  • Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
  • Cybersecurity and Emerging Technologies
  • Robotics and Autonomous Systems
  • Cloud Computing and Edge Technology
  • 5G/6G Networks and Beyond

Important Dates

Abstract Submission April 30, 2026
Full Paper Submission June 15, 2026
Peer Review Period June - August 2026
Acceptance Notification August 30, 2026
Camera Ready Deadline October 15, 2026

Paper Requirements

Paper Format

Papers must be submitted in PDF format. Maximum length: 8 pages including references. Use the provided paper template for formatting.

Language

Papers must be written in English. Ensure proper grammar, spelling, and technical writing standards throughout.

Originality

Papers must present original research. Plagiarism is strictly prohibited. All submitted papers will be checked for similarity.

Referencing

Use IEEE citation style for references. Include at least 15-20 relevant citations to demonstrate knowledge of the field.

Peer Review

All papers undergo a rigorous blind peer review process. Feedback will be provided to authors for revision.

Quality Standards

Papers must meet high standards of technical quality, clarity, and contribution to the field.

Submission Process

Follow these steps to submit your paper

1

Register or Login

Create an account or login to the submission portal with your credentials.

2

Fill in Paper Details

Provide paper title, abstract, keywords, and author information. Select the appropriate research track.

3

Upload Your Paper

Upload your PDF file. Ensure the file size is less than 10MB and follows the specified format.

4

Submit and Confirm

Review all information and submit your paper. You will receive a confirmation email with your submission ID.

5

Await Review

Your paper will be assigned to peer reviewers. You will receive feedback and decision notification.
